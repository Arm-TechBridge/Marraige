A Sacred Union

Honoring our roots, embracing our future.

The Nitchayathartham (The Promise)

Before the Agni, there was a promise. We exchanged rings formed from our own merged fingerprints—pure metal, devoid of stones. A testament to two true, unadorned selves finding harmony.

The Maangalyam (The Sacred Thread)

As the Getti Melam reaches its crescendo, we tie the sacred yellow thread. Three knots binding the mind, body, and soul. We invite you to witness this profound moment of union.

The Saptapadi (Seven Steps to Eternity)

With each of the seven steps around the sacred fire, we vow to nourish each other, grow in strength, prosper in wealth, share our joys, raise a noble family, live in harmony, and remain eternal friends. This is our endless journey.

Your blessings guide our path. 🙏
