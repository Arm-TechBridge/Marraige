Pillayar Suzhi (The Beginning)

With the blessings of Lord Ganesha and the guiding light of Lord Murugan, our families came together to weave our destinies. It was a beautiful culmination of tradition, respect, and mutual admiration.

Nitchayathartham (The Stoneless Promise)

We didn't want diamonds; we wanted truth. We exchanged custom rings forged purely from metal, bearing our intertwined half-and-half fingerprints. A pure, stoneless vow representing two unadorned souls finding complete harmony.

Maangalyam & Saptapadi

As the Nadaswaram plays and the Getti Melam echoes, we will tie the sacred yellow thread in the holy presence of Agni. With seven steps, Ragul and G will embark on an endless journey of shared sunrises and unwavering support.

The Grand Reception

Join us as we step into our new life together. An evening of joyous celebration, traditional feasts, and endless gratitude for the family and friends who have shaped our lives.

May the Divine shower grace upon us all. 🙏
